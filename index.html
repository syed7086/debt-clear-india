Under strict RBI Fair Practices Code guidelines, recovery agents can ONLY contact borrowers between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Agents are strictly prohibited from using abusive language, threatening arrest, contacting your family/colleagues/references, or visiting your workplace without prior written notice. Debt Clear India enforces these rules legally on your behalf.
When you settle a loan for less than the full amount, your credit report reflects a "Settled" status, which temporarily lowers your CIBIL score.
